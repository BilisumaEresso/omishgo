01 / THE OMISHGO EXPERIENCE MADE FOR LOCAL MARKETS
01 THE OPPORTUNITY

Good produce deserves
a better path to market.

Farmers work hard to grow valuable produce. Yet finding buyers, agreeing on fair prices, and coordinating transport can take days while middlemen take the lion's share.

OmishGo is designed to make direct trade transparent, simple, and reliable, strengthening the livelihood of local farming communities.

Our approach ↗

02 SIMPLE FROM THE START

From farm to buyer.
Fewer steps. More clarity.

One clear flow for listing produce, placing an order, and coordinating delivery across the Rift Valley.

01

Farmer lists produce

Snap a photo, set a price in Birr, describe the harvest. Listed in seconds and works smoothly on 2G.

↗
02

Buyer finds and orders

Buyers browse fresh produce by crop or location, order the needed amount by kuntaala, and message directly in-app.

↗
03

Delivery is coordinated

Track delivery status step-by-step. The local Union and logistics team keep all parties synchronized.

↗
04

Delivery is confirmed

Confirm receipt upon arrival, complete agreed payment, and leave trusted feedback on produce quality.
